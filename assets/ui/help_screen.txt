║                              HELP & CONTROLS                                 ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                              ║
║  MOVEMENT:                                                                   ║
║    Arrow Keys or WASD  - Move your character                                ║
║    Numpad 1-9          - Move in 8 directions (diagonal movement)           ║
║    5 or .              - Wait/Skip turn                                      ║
║                                                                              ║
║  ACTIONS:                                                                    ║
║    Space or Enter      - Attack adjacent enemy / Interact                    ║
║    i                   - Open/Close Inventory                                ║
║    c                   - Open Crafting Menu                                  ║
║    u                   - Use selected item                                   ║
║    d                   - Drop selected item                                  ║
║    e                   - Equip/Unequip item                                  ║
║    g                   - Pick up item from ground                            ║
║    >                   - Go down stairs                                      ║
║    <                   - Go up stairs                                        ║
║                                                                              ║
║  GAME:                                                                       ║
║    s                   - Save game                                           ║
║    l                   - Load game                                           ║
║    h or ?              - Show this help screen                               ║
║    Esc                 - Close menus / Pause                                 ║
║    q                   - Quit (with confirmation)                            ║
║                                                                              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                              GAME MECHANICS                                  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                              ║
║  COMBAT:                                                                     ║
║    - Move into enemies to attack them                                        ║
║    - Each attack has a chance to hit based on accuracy                       ║
║    - Critical hits deal double damage                                        ║
║    - Defense reduces incoming damage                                         ║
║    - Defeat enemies to gain XP and signal drops                              ║
║                                                                              ║
║  SIGNAL CRAFTING:                                                            ║
║    - Collect signals from defeated enemies                                   ║
║    - Open crafting menu (C) to view recipes                                  ║
║    - Combine signals to create powerful effects                              ║
║    - Some signals are consumed, others are reusable (marked with *)          ║
║    - Experiment to discover new recipes!                                     ║
║                                                                              ║
║  ITEMS:                                                                      ║
║    - Consumables: Use once for immediate effect (healing, buffs)             ║
║    - Equipment: Equip for permanent stat bonuses                             ║
║    - Signals: Used in crafting recipes                                       ║
║    - Quest Items: Required for special objectives                            ║
║                                                                              ║
║  STATUS EFFECTS:                                                             ║
║    - Buffs increase your stats temporarily                                   ║
║    - Debuffs reduce your stats or deal damage over time                      ║
║    - Check status panel to see active effects                                ║
║    - Effects tick down each turn                                             ║
║                                                                              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                              ║
║  SYMBOLS:                                                                    ║
║    @  - You (the player)                                                     ║
║    p  - Corrupted Packet (enemy)                                             ║
║    g  - Signal Glitch (enemy)                                                ║
║    !  - Item (potion, consumable)                                            ║
║    *  - Signal (crafting material)                                           ║
║    [  - Equipment (armor, weapon)                                            ║
║    >  - Stairs down                                                          ║
║    <  - Stairs up                                                            ║
║    #  - Wall                                                                 ║
║    .  - Floor                                                                ║
║    +  - Door                                                                 ║
║                                                                              ║
║  Press any key to close this help screen...                                  ║
